# Safe AI Guy — Agentic AI Risk & Control Matrix (RCM)

> **Standard:** Governance-as-Code for Autonomous Agents  
> **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Scope:** MIT AI Risk Repository v3 (our classification: 5 of 24 subdomains decidable at the tool-call boundary, 3 in part), OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (2026), GovTech Singapore Agentic Risk & Capability Framework (ARC), NIST AI RMF 1.0

## Executive Summary & Scope Boundary

The MIT AI Risk Repository defines 24 subdomains and does not classify them by runtime decidability. In our classification (docs/agentic_risk_screener.md, section 2), 5 subdomains are decidable at the tool-call boundary (2.1, 2.2, 5.2, 7.3, 7.4) and 3 in part (4.2, 7.2, 7.6). runtime_decidable=false marks rows whose control lives outside the tool-call hook (container isolation, pre-install scanning). GovTech ARC IDs were checked against ARC's risks.yaml and controls.yaml.

### Core Governance Domains

- **`AG-DAT`**: Data & Privacy Boundary
- **`AG-SEC`**: Adversarial & Endpoint Security
- **`AG-SAF`**: Action Safety & Blast-Radius Control
- **`AG-REL`**: Tool & Execution Reliability
- **`AG-ACC`**: Accountability, Audit & Supply Chain
- **`AG-MIS`**: Cyber & Catastrophic Misuse Prevention
